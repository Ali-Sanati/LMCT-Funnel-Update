# LMCT+ SA Fuel + $1M funnel direction

## Mode

Persuade.

## Argument

1. South Australians can buy 12 months of wholesale fuel access at Edwardstown for $199 per year. Proof: the single offer, price, renewal and location in the first viewport.
2. Station activity can earn entries in the current $1,000,000 giveaway. Proof: the two exact rates and a working fuel-litre estimator.
3. Fuel access remains useful beyond this featured giveaway. Proof: access-duration and promotion-duration language kept separate.
4. The LMCT+ app helps customers follow relevant activity. Proof: the supplied app image, with a precise disclaimer excluding pictured prices, rewards and unrelated promotions.
5. The current giveaway ends 29 October 2026. Proof: a date-led campaign chapter without an unverified countdown.
6. The annual package is one clear decision. Proof: a single $199 offer repeated only at the final close.

## Subject inventory

Edwardstown; South Road; petrol canopy blue; forecourt lighting; 50-litre milestones; $20 shop purchases; accumulated litres across visits; annual access; $199; 29 October 2026; LMCT+ app progress; current $1,000,000 promotion; petrol station shop.

## Idea

On this page, the forecourt journey becomes a bold progress route from fill-up to entries, with the current $1,000,000 prize as a single electric-blue destination rather than a generic giveaway collage.

## First viewport

A compact petrol-branded header leads into a split navy campaign field: short fuel-first headline and precise offer copy on the left, the supplied Edwardstown station image on the right, and a full-width blue prize strip anchoring the current promotion. The $199 annual price, automatic renewal and fuel-sold-separately terms are visible with the first CTA.

## Visitor path

Offer clarity → exact earning mechanics → interactive 50 L progress example → app context → current promotion date → FAQ → final offer and CTA.

## Signature interaction

The fuel-progress control lets a visitor adjust litres and immediately see confirmed 50 L bonus-entry milestones. It uses real campaign arithmetic, works by keyboard and touch, and has a readable static fallback.

## Composition contract

Use varied full-width fields and asymmetric splits, not a procession of same-size cards. Headlines stay to two lines at each breakpoint. The one-package offer is a price ledger, the earning mechanics are a two-part route, the campaign is a high-contrast typographic field, and the FAQ is a quiet editorial close. Motion is limited to control feedback, FAQ state, smooth anchor navigation, one restrained image reveal and short, content-specific transform cues on key proof points where supported; no blanket fades or delayed reading.
