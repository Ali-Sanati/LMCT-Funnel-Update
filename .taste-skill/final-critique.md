ROUND 3

Seen in one second: A bold LMCT+ Petrol campaign: $199 a year for Edwardstown fuel access, with a current $1M giveaway chance.

Idea (one sentence): A printed annual-offer campaign translated into a crisp blue-and-navy digital sales page, using the real Edwardstown station and app as evidence.

Live thing: The fuel-progress control turns accumulated litres into bonus entries and remaining progress using the confirmed 50 L rate.

Layout words by section: hero=campaign billboard / offer=annual plan ledger / earning=paired rate posters plus location evidence / progress=explanatory worksheet / app=product proof / campaign=editorial prize field / FAQ=quiet disclosure list / final=compact close.

Scores: design 9.0 · usability 9.3 · creativity 8.7 · content 9.5 → overall 9.1.

Caps applied: none.

Top three problems, screenshot and fix:

1. The early build's repeated white cards made the offer feel generic. In the final desktop screenshot, the $199 plan is a single editorial composition and the earning rates are the only paired panels. Fix: replaced repeated card grids with alternating full-width navy, white and campaign-blue sections.
2. The app image can visibly contain prices and promotions that do not belong to this package. In the final tablet screenshot it is treated as product proof, not offer copy. Fix: paired it with only two confirmed tracking statements and a direct disclaimer that pictured prices, savings, rewards and other promotions are not inclusions.
3. A persistent mobile action could cover headings, controls or the footer. In the final mobile screenshots it appears when useful and clears the slider, FAQ controls, final offer and footer. Fix: added intersection guards plus safe-area spacing and a 56 px tap target.

Remaining warnings reviewed:

- Size-count: the 18 reported computed sizes are values produced by five responsive `clamp()` roles across three viewport measurements, not 18 authored text styles. The rendered hierarchy is consistent, so no change was made.
- Grey-body: primary running copy uses full ink. The softer colour is intentionally limited to billing notes, captions and disclaimers; all measured combinations remain readable.
- No footage: no verified LMCT+ Edwardstown campaign video was supplied. A decorative or unrelated clip would weaken trust, so the supplied station and app imagery remain the visual foundation.
- Little motion: 10 elements move across a 6.8-screen page. This is deliberate: the brief asks for restrained motion and rejects elaborate entrances. The page uses one station-image signature reveal, short content-specific transform cues, functional slider/FAQ feedback and no hidden-by-default copy.

Taste direction: `editorial-report`, seed `1003941996`. Kept its fixed-axis hierarchy, solid colour fields and editorial image crops. Declined its unrelated imagery and static-list bias because the supplied LMCT+ assets and accessible interactive FAQ better serve the campaign brief.
