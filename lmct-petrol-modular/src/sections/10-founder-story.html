A note from Adrian

Why we created LMCT+ Petrol

Fuel is a regular cost for Australian households, and traditional loyalty discounts can feel small. LMCT+ Petrol was created to pass member pricing through at participating locations while keeping the membership simple.

The problem

Four big companies sell a large share of fuel in Australia. The source copy frames LMCT+ Petrol as a practical response to high pump prices and small loyalty-card discounts.

What LMCT+ built

The existing founder copy says LMCT+ built a real petrol station, buys from major suppliers, and makes money from membership rather than the pump.

The member value

The ask is simple: join, use the app, and see whether the member pricing works for your fuel routine.
