# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, scoped CSS and vanilla JavaScript for ClickFunnels, plus a standalone HTML preview.

## Users

South Australian customers evaluating whether to buy one annual LMCT+ package for use at LMCT+ Petrol Edwardstown.

## Product Purpose

The funnel must make a single annual offer easy to understand and act on: AUD $199 provides 12 months of wholesale fuel pricing access at Edwardstown, while the qualifying purchase and subsequent station activity can earn entries in the featured promotion.

## Positioning

The offer combines practical, location-specific fuel access with promotion entry earning tied to everyday station activity.

## Operating Context

Customers buy fuel separately at LMCT+ Petrol Edwardstown. Qualifying fuel litres accumulate across transactions, and every 50 L earns one bonus entry. Every $20 spent in the petrol station shop earns one bonus entry. Customers can use the LMCT+ app to view relevant activity and progress, subject to the live app experience and promotion rules.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One package: AUD $199 per year, automatically renewing annually until cancelled.
- Each annual payment provides 12 months of wholesale fuel pricing access.
- Fuel is purchased separately; the annual payment does not buy free fuel.
- Joining includes immediate entries in the current $1,000,000 giveaway, but the quantity is not confirmed.
- Featured giveaway ends 29 October 2026; exact closing time and timezone are not confirmed.
- The 12-month access period is not the promotion duration.
- Future featured promotions can differ. Current entries are not promised to carry forward.
- Supermarket access, fixed fuel prices, unlimited fills, grocery credit, partner discounts, tier benefits and entry multipliers are outside this offer unless separately confirmed.
- Open launch decisions: initial purchase entry quantity, checkout destination or native checkout selector, official promotion terms URL, exact closing timestamp/timezone, and whether shop spend accumulates across transactions.

## Brand Commitments

LMCT+ styling uses Barlow Semi Condensed for bold italic uppercase headings, Jost for readable body copy, navy `#07142D`, action blue `#1D5FB8`, bright blue `#1D8FFF`, pale blue `#D9ECFF`, soft blue-white `#F7FBFF`, white surfaces, approved LMCT+ logo assets, restrained rounded panels and concise Australian-English copy.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the practical value of fuel access; use the promotion as a clear chance, never a guaranteed outcome.
- Keep price, renewal, duration, location and fuel-purchased-separately terms next to the action.
- State entry mechanics precisely and do not fill unresolved policy gaps with invented rules.
- Keep the funnel focused on this single South Australian package.
- Preserve accessible, responsive behaviour inside ClickFunnels without leaking global styles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings and controls, visible keyboard focus, readable terms, 44px-or-larger mobile tap targets, reduced-motion support, and a mobile sticky CTA that respects safe areas and never covers the footer or form controls.
