Frequently asked questions

Here is what you need to know about eligibility, claiming your credit and using it at LMCT+ Petrol Edwardstown.

What happens if my fill up costs more than $50?

The system will apply your $50 fuel credit first. You can pay the remaining balance by EFTPOS or cash.

Who qualifies as an existing member?

Existing member claims are limited to members who were active before 1 September. Memberships started on or after that date do not qualify through the existing member offer.

Are South Australian BCF and Save and Win members eligible?

Yes. South Australian members who previously joined through BCF or Save and Win are recognised as previous members for this offer, subject to the other eligibility requirements.

Which membership plans qualify for the $50 fuel credit?

The fuel credit is available with the $99 annual membership. The $20 per month and $9.99 per month plans are excluded from this offer.

Can I get a refund on the $99 membership?

No. The $99 annual membership is non refundable. Manual refunds will not be processed during the promotional window.

How long do I have to claim or use the $50 fuel credit?

Existing members have 72 hours to claim the $50 fuel credit. New members have six months to use their credit.
