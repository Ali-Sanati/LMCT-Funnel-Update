Why we created LMCT+ Petrol

Fuel is a regular cost for Australian households, and traditional loyalty discounts can feel small. LMCT+ Petrol was created to pass member pricing through at participating locations while keeping the membership simple.

The member value

How does the membership work?

Join online, access your LMCT+ membership through the app, then use it at participating fuel and everyday offer locations.

How do fuel savings work?

The calculator estimates savings from the difference between the entered local pump price and LMCT+ price, multiplied by tank size and fill frequency. Actual prices and savings vary.

What grocery offers are included?

The supplied copy references grocery and everyday member pricing. Use the current membership terms and in-app offers for exact participating grocery details.

When does membership activate?

Is the $99 fee recurring?

Can members cancel?
