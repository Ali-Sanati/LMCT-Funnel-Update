Frequently asked questions

Here is what you need to know about joining and accessing member value at LMCT+ Market Deer Park.

What is included with each membership plan?

Choose Save On Fuel + Groceries for A$99/year or Save + Win for A$199/year. Both plans include 12 months of LMCT+ Market membership, member grocery pricing, eligible fuel savings and exclusive offers. Save + Win also includes automatic entry into every LMCT+ giveaway.

Where can I access my membership benefits?

Access member grocery pricing at LMCT+ Market Deer Park, 8 Hatchlands Drive, Deer Park VIC 3023. Eligible fuel savings and exclusive offers are available at participating locations and are subject to their individual terms.

How does Save + Win work?

Save + Win includes the same eligible member savings plus automatic entry into every LMCT+ giveaway while your membership is active, subject to the applicable membership and giveaway terms.

Do I need to buy groceries in bulk?

No. Enjoy member pricing on everyday grocery quantities without buying in bulk or purchasing wholesale sized packs.

What other membership benefits can I access?

Your membership includes eligible fuel savings and exclusive member offers where available. Participating locations, eligible products and individual offer terms may vary.

When does LMCT+ Market Deer Park open?

The exact opening date is October 13th at 10AM. In store grocery membership benefits will be available once the store opens.

How long does the membership last?

Each option provides 12 months of membership. Renewal and ongoing membership are subject to the applicable membership terms.

What if I am already an LMCT+ member?

Before purchasing another membership, email support@lmctplus.com to confirm whether your current plan includes Market access and whether another annual plan is right for you.

Can I get a refund on my membership?

Change of mind refunds are not available on either annual plan. Your rights to refunds and other remedies under Australian Consumer Law remain unaffected. Please review the membership and offer terms before purchasing.
