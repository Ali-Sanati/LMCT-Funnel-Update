Reviewed: 20 October 2026. Status: website-derived working reference; unresolved claims require business confirmation.

## 1. Business model

**OBSERVED:** LMCT+ sells access to a membership, savings and rewards platform. Its benefits span partner discounts, fuel pricing, groceries/markets, member events and promotional giveaway entries attached to eligible purchases or memberships. Membership is the product described by the site; giveaway entries are presented as included/free benefits. S1–S4.

**OBSERVED:** The operator named in membership terms is Xclusive Tech Pty Ltd trading as LMCT Plus, ABN 93 627 297 474. S6 §1.

| Commercial component | What the pages establish | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Loyalty subscriptions | Entry, Premium and Elite; monthly and yearly options. Ongoing debits until cancellation/termination are stated in membership terms. | S4; S6 §12 |
| Saver subscriptions/packages | Everyday Saver and Saver + Win are annual-only on home/fuel; a differently described Petrol Saver appears on membership. Product identity is unresolved. | S1, S2, S4 |
| One-off packages | Time-limited platform access; promotional entries tied to the official campaign link used. | S6 §§4–10 |
| Fuel and retail | Membership connects to fuel-site pricing and grocery shopping; actual retail margins and ownership arrangements are not disclosed. | S2, S3 |
| Merchandise | Store links and tier-specific merchandise discounts. | S1, S4 |
| In-app ecosystem | Coins, EXP, games and marketplace features. Purchased Coins are described separately from major giveaway entries. | S5 §2 |

**INFERRED:** Everyday savings create repeat utility; promotions attract attention; accumulating entries and rewards encourage retention; higher tiers offer reasons to upgrade; annual billing encourages a longer commitment. The site does not establish the revenue mix, profitability, partner commission model, customer acquisition cost or lifetime value.

## 4. Markets

S3 presents supermarkets as forthcoming, with in-store and online shopping, member grocery pricing and possible coins, XP and giveaway entries on eligible purchases. It does not give a launch date, delivery area, store address, earning rate, minimum spend, redemption value or comprehensive exclusions. S1's markets FAQ also describes a future nationwide rollout.

The Get Early Access CTA goes to the Saver signup route; it is not evidence that a free waitlist is being offered. No form was submitted and the destination checkout was not audited.

S5's market price-beat section describes a conditional 10% reduction from a verified competitor price, not a blanket 10% saving on all groceries: identical product, regular shelf price at physical Coles/Woolworths in the same state, stock at both stores, claim before payment, exclusions for promotions and online-only prices. This policy's existence does not verify that a particular market is open.

## 5. Membership and promotion rules

- Loyalty subscriptions continue until cancelled/terminated. Trials and sign-up bonuses have their own conditions; S6 §27 describes charging after a trial unless cancelled.
- S6 distinguishes one-off access packages from loyalty. Listed access periods: Newbie 1 day; Entry/Starter 2 days; Bronze 7 days; Silver 28 days; Gold 182 days; Platinum 365 days. Prices and campaign entry counts are not established by that table.
- One-off access days can accumulate. Where both loyalty and one-off access exist, one-off days are used after loyalty ends. S6 §§6–7.
- One-off promotional entries apply to the specific promotion associated with the official purchase link and promotion period, not every promotion. S6 §§8–10.
- Loyalty accumulation is described in terms of continuous membership and current tier, but its worked example conflicts with the stated rate. Treat the algorithm as disputed. S6 §§13,15.
- Membership terms list milestone bonuses: 5 entries at six months; 10 at subsequent six-month milestones, for one selected promotion, claimed through support with proof. Do not assume automatic application. S6 §18.
- Tier-change requests take effect next billing cycle according to S6 §19. Annual proration and other implementation details are not established here.
- S5 describes account eligibility as 18+. S6 describes Australian-resident promotional eligibility with employee/family exclusions and jurisdictional restrictions. Individual promotion terms still govern eligibility; do not promise universal entry eligibility.
- Membership cancellation and marketing unsubscribe are separate actions. S5 §7; S6 §22.
- S5 §4 distinguishes change-of-mind refusals from consumer-guarantee remedies. Later blanket non-refund statements need consistent qualification; this is a copy/policy audit, not a ruling on enforceability.

## 7. Safe AI knowledge model

Use the following concepts separately: membership product, billing option, benefit, location, promotion, entry entitlement, reward unit, policy, source claim and conflict.

For every claim retain: claim ID; subject/product; predicate; value and unit; source URL and section; observed date; effective dates if explicitly supplied; geographic and eligibility scope; status (observed/inferred/conflicted/approved); and conflicting claim IDs. Observation date is not an effective date.

Useful relationships include product HAS_BILLING_OPTION, product GRANTS_BENEFIT, benefit VALID_AT_LOCATION, purchase QUALIFIES_FOR_PROMOTION, rule SOURCED_FROM and claim CONFLICTS_WITH. Preserve the competing claims; do not collapse them into one value.

AI operating rules:

1. Read the approved business reference and campaign brief before generating copy.
2. Keep Saver, loyalty and one-off packages distinct until an explicit product mapping is approved.
3. Never translate partner-network access percentages into purchase discount percentages.
4. Never generalize a station benefit, pump price, grocery launch or location-specific offer nationally.
5. Keep major giveaway entries, mini entries, fuel-listed entries, Coins and EXP separate.
6. Do not use unlimited savings to infer unlimited fuel volume/frequency.
7. Do not promise permanent entries after cancellation or select a restoration/retry rule from conflicting policies.
8. Preserve qualifications on eligibility, actual savings, launch timing and refunds.
9. Treat popup discounts, deadlines and multipliers as separate dated campaigns; do not promote them to permanent business rules.
10. Do not assume a later-looking page supersedes another; require an approved rule owner/effective date when sources conflict.

## 9. Visual identity and reusable funnel CSS

Added 20 October 2026 from browser-computed styles and readable stylesheet rules on S1–S4. This is an extraction of the website implementation, not an official brand manual. EXACT identifies observed values; ADAPTED identifies recommended funnel defaults. Source CSS contains multiple page-specific systems; do not treat all variations as one mandatory token set. Desktop styles and responsive source rules were inspected; a separate mobile viewport rendering was not performed.

### Overview

The recurring identity uses heavy, condensed, italic uppercase headings; clear sans-serif supporting text; electric and deep blues; pale blue/white panels; rounded cards and pill actions. Some website heroes use navy backgrounds, imagery and gradients, while pricing and explanatory sections use light surfaces. Markets introduces lime green. Preserve the typography, logo and blue hierarchy when creating a light-background funnel; a dark hero is not required to express this brand.

Recommended funnel treatment: white or very pale blue canvas, navy headings, deep-blue primary buttons, bright-blue highlights, restrained card shadows, and one clear primary action. Treat that as an adaptation for funnels, not a claim that the website is entirely light-themed.

### Layout

EXACT: Fuel hero inner width caps at 1360px; explanatory sections cap at 1240px. Desktop outer gutters are 24px, becoming 18px on small fuel layouts. Several content grids collapse at 820px. Navigation changes at 900px; many detailed adjustments occur at 640px. Compact homepage membership cards also have 880px/760px variants. These are component-specific breakpoints, not a single shared framework.

EXACT: Compact membership cards use `padding: clamp(24px, 2.4vw, 32px)`. Fuel journey section padding is `clamp(96px, 10vw, 150px) 24px`. Such large marketing-section spacing is useful for the main website but unnecessary for a short urgency funnel.

ADAPTED: Cap ordinary funnel sections at 1120px; use 720px for focused copy/checkout sections. Use 18–24px outer gutters and 48–88px vertical section spacing. Stack offer cards on mobile rather than hiding a plan off-screen. These are recommended values supplied below, not extracted source constants.

### Elevation & depth

EXACT light-card shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(29,95,184,.07)` on the Saver panel. EXACT Saver button shadow: `0 10px 22px rgba(29,95,184,.18)`. Larger photographic cards use stronger blue-toned shadows and subtle inset highlights. Compact tier cards are flat at rest.

ADAPTED: Use the light-card shadow for selected panels; avoid applying glows, gradients and shadows to every nested element. Use a border or background change to distinguish plans before adding more depth.

### Shapes

EXACT: Compact tier cards use 16px corners, Saver panels 17px, membership-page cards 20px, and larger value cards 25px. Many CTAs use 999px pill corners; homepage hero actions change to 13px corners below 640px.

ADAPTED: Use 16px cards, 12px inputs and pill CTAs consistently within one funnel. Keep the price, billing frequency and CTA grouped; do not let an oversized badge cover price or terms.

### Components and interaction

- **Primary CTA:** S1 Saver uses deep-blue fill, white text, Barlow 800 italic uppercase, .08em tracking, 48px minimum height and full width. S2 actions use 54px minimum height. The starter adapts this to 54px, larger readable text and wrapping labels.
- **Motion:** Source actions frequently use 220ms and `cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)`, a 2px upward hover shift, and a small press response. Keep reduced-motion support and scope hover effects to hover-capable devices.
- **Focus:** Source pages use 3px focus outlines with offsets. The starter uses deep blue on light surfaces and white on dark/blue panels for clear keyboard focus.
- **Pricing:** S1 compact summary uses white Entry, blue Premium and pale-blue Elite. S4 has a different light-card system. Choose one scheme per funnel; do not mix both arbitrarily.
- **Forms:** The inspected pages do not establish a complete checkout field system. The starter input/error treatment is ADAPTED: visible labels, 16px input text, 52px minimum height and explicit error text. It does not implement validation or payment behavior.
- **Countdown:** Use tabular numerals for stable widths. The CSS below supplies appearance only; use the confirmed campaign deadline/timezone and existing countdown logic.
- **Accordions:** The starter uses native details/summary for a usable no-JavaScript FAQ. Do not style a non-interactive div to look like a control.

### Do's and don'ts for future AI builds

- Do use the observed font hierarchy; keep body copy upright and readable.
- Do use one coherent page variant and a light surface default for the starter.
- Do keep business facts from sections 1–8 separate from appearance. A styled card does not resolve its offer rules.
- Do use real approved product/vehicle images and logo assets; preserve their aspect ratio.
- Do keep CTA focus visible, allow labels to wrap and account for long prices/plan names.
- Do scope CSS to a wrapper and test desktop/mobile layouts inside the actual funnel platform before launch.
- Don't copy generic `body`, `button`, `a`, `input` or `*` rules out of the website into ClickFunnels without scoping.
- Don't import the full site bundle merely to style a funnel; it includes unrelated navigation, page-specific rules and animation dependencies.
- Don't copy scroll-reveal opacity/transform rules without their JavaScript; content can remain invisible.
- Don't treat Barlow and Eurostile as interchangeable assets, or load every font for a small page.
- Don't turn the website's very small notes, tight hero line-heights or heavy photographic overlays into mandatory brand rules.
- Don't add fake scarcity, new promotional claims or altered checkout behavior as part of a visual styling pass.
